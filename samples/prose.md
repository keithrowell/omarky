The first paragraph of a long essay should set the tone: calm, even, and readable at length. It carries **bold emphasis**, a little *italic colour*, and some `inline code` so we can judge how the inline styles sit inside a measure of about sixty-eight characters.

A second paragraph follows. Good typography disappears; you notice only that reading is easy. The line height, the measure and the spacing between paragraphs all conspire to keep your eye moving without effort, line after line.
